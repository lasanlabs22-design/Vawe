# Mirror studio assets

Created with the built-in imagegen tool. Source photographs remain unchanged.

- `public/images/mirror-studio/room.png`: edited from the client's blue-tile bathroom mouse mirror reference. Prompt: remove all VAWE logos, promotional wording, phone number and pink marks; remove the mirror and its glow; reconstruct continuous navy tiles; preserve the basin, vanity, brass hardware, plants, marble, shower, lighting and portrait composition. No text or mirror remains in the backdrop.
- `public/images/mirror-studio/reflection.png`: generated reflection plate. Prompt: straight-on luxury bathroom opposite wall, navy vertical ceramic tiles, marble shower at right, slim brass hardware, warm ceiling lights and subtle plant; no mirror, people, camera, text or logos; sharp portrait architectural photograph.

The mirror shapes are SVG silhouettes interpreted from the supplied reference collection, plus round and arch options. All fourteen share the same room and reflection plate. Lighting, frame and brightness are rendered independently so the room remains unchanged when selecting a mirror. This is an illustrative configurator rather than a ray-traced reflection.
